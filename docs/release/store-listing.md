## Description

Copy everything between the lines.

```
Around The World is pickup soccer for the Bay Area.

You host a game. People nearby find it, claim a spot, and show up. That is the whole app.

FIND A GAME
Open Matches. These are real Bay Area listings — San Francisco, Oakland, San Jose, the Peninsula, the East Bay, North Bay. Filter by day, how far you will travel, time of day, and how serious it is (Casual, Intermediate, Baller, or Open to All). Flip to the map when you want to see the field.

CLAIM A SPOT
Open a game, see who is hosting, and claim a spot. If it is full, join the waitlist.

HOST FOR FREE
Hit the + button, drop a pin on a Bay Area field, and publish. Hosting costs nothing. If you collect a few dollars at the field for lights or turf, put that on the listing so people know. The app never takes a payment. No Apple Pay, no cards, no in-app purchases.

YOUR PROFILE
Sign in with Apple. Add your city, position, and a short bio. My Games is the list of sessions you hosted or claimed.

BAY AREA ONLY, ON PURPOSE
Version 1 is the nine counties: San Francisco, San Mateo, Santa Clara, Alameda, Contra Costa, Marin, Solano, Napa, and Sonoma. You can download the app from anywhere. You cannot post a game outside that area.

This is pickup among people you might not know. Use public fields. Tell someone where you are going.
```
