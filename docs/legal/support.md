# Around The World — Support

Around The World is a pickup soccer app for the San Francisco Bay Area. Hosting is free. There are **no in-app payments**.

## Common questions

**How do I sign in?**  
Use **Sign in with Apple**. Hide My Email is fine.

**How do I find a game?**  
Open Matches. Listings are Bay Area pickup games with an open spot. You can filter by day, distance, time of day, and skill.

**How do I claim a spot?**  
Open a game and tap Claim Spot. You must be signed in. If it is full you can join the waitlist.

**How do I host?**  
Tap the + button, pick a Bay Area venue, fill in the game, and publish. You cannot post a game outside the nine counties.

**Is there a fee?**  
The app is free. Hosting is free. If a card shows a price, that is what the host is collecting **at the field**. We never charge your Apple ID or a card in the app.

**How do I change my profile?**  
Avatar (top of the app) → Edit. Name, city, age, position, and a 200-character bio. Photos stay on this phone until photo upload exists.

**How do I report someone?**  
Open the game → **Report this game**. Choose a reason. We review reports by hand.

**How do I block someone?**  
Open the game → **Block host**. You will stop seeing each other’s games.

**How do I delete my account?**  
Settings → **Delete account**. This is permanent. Apple requires this button. It deletes your Around The World account and the games and signups attached to it.

**The legal pages in Settings say “not published yet.”**  
That means the public links have not been pasted into this build. Once they are, Privacy Policy and Help will open in Safari.

**I am not in the Bay Area.**  
Version 1 only supports the nine-county San Francisco Bay Area. You can download the app, but you cannot post a game outside that area.

## Something broken

Tell us:

1. What you tapped
2. What you expected
3. What you saw instead
4. iPhone model and iOS version

Do not send passwords, Apple ID passwords, or screenshots that show tokens or keys.

## Safety

This is pickup soccer among strangers. Use public fields, tell someone where you are going, and leave if a situation feels wrong. The app is a listing tool, not a background check.
