# Privacy Policy

Around The World (“the app”) is a pickup soccer app for the San Francisco Bay Area. It is operated by **Auner Vasquez** as an individual developer (“we”, “us”).

This policy is written in plain language. It describes what the iPhone app and its server collect, why, and how to delete it.

## Who this is for

The app is intended for people **13 and older**. It is not directed at children under 13. If you are under 13, do not create an account.

Version 1 is **Bay Area only**. Games can only be posted inside the nine-county San Francisco Bay Area.

## What the app is (and is not)

Around The World helps people find and host pickup soccer games. Hosting is free. If a game lists a price, that is information only — you pay the host at the field if you choose to play.

**The app does not take payments.** There is no Apple Pay, no credit cards, no Stripe, and no in-app purchases in this version.

## Information we collect

### Account (Sign in with Apple)

You sign in with Apple. We receive:

- A unique Apple / login id (so we know it is you next time)
- A name, if Apple sends one
- An email address — this may be Apple’s Hide My Email relay (`…@privaterelay.appleid.com`). That is fine. We store whatever address Apple gives us.

We do **not** post to your Apple feed. We do **not** get your Apple password.

### Profile you type

You may add:

- Display name
- City
- Age
- Favorite position
- A short bio (200 characters or less)
- A profile photo *on this phone only* in this version — photos are not uploaded to our servers yet

Bios are visible to other people using the app.

### Games you host or join

If you host a game we store the title, venue name, neighborhood, kickoff time, format, skill level, player cap, optional notes (500 characters or less), and the map coordinates of the field.

If you claim a spot we store that you joined or waitlisted that game.

Game listings (including host notes) are visible to people browsing the app, including people who are not signed in.

### Location

The app asks for **location while you use it** so it can show nearby Bay Area games and so you can drop a pin when you host.

- We do not track you in the background.
- We do not sell location data.
- Field coordinates for a game you host are stored with that game (they are the pitch, not your home).
- You can deny location. Browse still works; “near me” and pin-drop hosting will be limited.

### Reports and blocks

If you report a game or a player, we store that you sent the report, what you reported, the reason, and any details you typed. If you block someone, we store that relationship so you stop seeing each other’s games.

### Device and technical data

The server sees a normal web request: IP address, time, and that you are using the app. We use this to keep the service up, rate-limit abuse, and debug crashes. We do not run advertising SDKs.

## What we do not collect

- Payment information (no payments in this version)
- Contacts, photos on our servers, microphone, health, or browsing history
- Precise location as a live track of where you walk
- Advertising identifiers for ads (there are no ads)

## How we use the information

- To create and keep your account
- To show games, let you claim a spot, and let you host
- To show your profile to other players
- To hide people you blocked
- To review reports of abuse
- To operate, secure, and fix the app
- To comply with Apple’s rules (including in-app account deletion)

We do **not** sell your information. We do **not** use it for third-party advertising.

## Who we share it with

We share only what is needed to run the app:

| Who | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **Apple** | Sign in with Apple, App Store, MapKit maps |
| **Supabase** | Login records and the hosted database |
| **Our hosting provider** (for example Railway, when the server is live) | Runs the API |

Other players can see public game cards and the profile fields you filled in. They cannot see your email.

If the law requires it, we may disclose information to a court or regulator.

## How long we keep it

We keep your account and related games, signups, reports, and blocks until you delete the account, or until we shut the service down.

If you delete your account in Settings, we delete the Around The World account on our server and the games and signups attached to it. Sign in with Apple again later will create a new empty account.

Apple and Supabase keep their own records of the login itself under their policies.

## Your choices

- **Sign out** in Settings. The app forgets the session on this phone. Your games stay on the server.
- **Delete account** in Settings. This is the in-app deletion Apple requires. It cannot be undone.
- **Location** — allow or deny in iPhone Settings → Around The World.
- **Hide My Email** — use it. We will store the relay address.
- **Report / Block** on a game’s page if someone posts abusive text.

## Security

We store the login token in the iPhone Keychain. The server checks that token before it will change your account, report, block, or delete you. No password is stored by this app.

No method is perfect. Do not put phone numbers, home addresses, or payment details in a bio or in game notes.

## California / Bay Area users

If you are in California you can ask what personal information we have, ask us to delete it (Delete Account does this for the app), and ask us not to sell it (we do not sell it). Use the support page to reach us.
